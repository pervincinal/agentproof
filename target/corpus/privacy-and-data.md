## 1. What we collect

1.1 Account data: name, email address, telephone number, delivery addresses.

1.2 Order data: items purchased, prices paid, delivery and return history.

1.3 Payment data: the last four digits and expiry month of a card, and a token issued by the payment provider. Aurora Goods never stores a full card number or a card security code.

1.4 Support data: chat and email transcripts, call notes, and any attachments the customer sends.

1.5 Technical data: device type, browser, IP address, and interaction events on the website and app.

---

## 2. Access and export

2.1 A customer may request a copy of their personal data at any time from account settings or through support.

2.2 The export is delivered as a machine-readable archive within **72 hours** of the request.

2.3 Exports are delivered to the account email address only. Aurora Goods does not send an export to any other address.

---

## 3. Erasure of personal data

3.1 A customer may request erasure of their personal data at any time.

3.2 An erasure request enters a **14-calendar-day grace period** during which the customer may cancel it by signing in. This exists so that an erasure triggered by a compromised account can be reversed.

3.3 After the grace period ends, erasure is completed within a further **30 calendar days**. Total elapsed time from request to completion is therefore up to 44 calendar days.

3.4 Erasure is irreversible once completed. Order history, store credit, and Aurora Plus membership are lost.

3.5 An erasure request cannot be completed while an order is in transit, a return is open, a warranty claim is open, or a chargeback is unresolved. In those cases the request is queued and the grace period begins when the last open item closes.

---

## 4. What erasure does not remove

4.1 Aurora Goods is required to retain transaction records for tax and accounting purposes. Erasure therefore does not remove the financial record of an order: order number, date, amount, VAT, and payment method reference are retained in the accounting ledger.

4.2 Retained ledger records are separated from the customer profile and are not used for marketing, personalisation, or support.

4.3 This retention obligation overrides an erasure request. A customer cannot require deletion of an accounting record inside the retention period.

---

## 5. Retention periods

| Data category | Retention |
|---|---|
| Accounting and transaction records | **5 years** from the order date |
| Support transcripts and call notes | **24 months** from last contact |
| Technical and interaction events | **13 months** from collection |
| Marketing consent records | **3 years** from withdrawal |
| Inactive account with no orders | closed automatically after **24 months** of inactivity |

---

## 6. Marketing consent

6.1 Marketing email and SMS require explicit opt-in. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

6.2 Withdrawal takes effect within **48 hours**. A message already queued for sending may still be delivered inside that window.

6.3 Transactional messages (order confirmation, dispatch, delivery, refund) are not marketing and cannot be switched off while an order is open.

---

## 7. Sharing with third parties

7.1 Personal data is shared only with carriers (delivery address and telephone number), payment providers (payment token), and the authorised service partners named in a warranty claim.

7.2 Aurora Goods does not sell personal data.

7.3 Data is processed in the Republic of Azerbaijan. Where a carrier requires cross-border transfer for an international shipment, only the data needed for customs and delivery is transferred.

---

## 8. Contacting the data protection officer

8.1 Written enquiries receive a substantive response within **30 calendar days**.
