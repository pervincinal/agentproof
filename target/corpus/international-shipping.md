> **Document ID:** AG-POL-INT
> **Version:** 1.6
> **Effective from:** 2026-07-01
> **Supersedes:** v1.5 (in force 2025-11-01 through 2026-06-30)
> **Scope:** orders delivered to addresses outside the Republic of Azerbaijan
> **Owner:** Logistics
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-30
> **Related documents:** `shipping-and-delivery.md`, `returns-and-refunds.md`, `payments-and-billing.md`, `warranty-policy.md`

---

## 1. Scope

1.1 This document governs every order whose delivery address is outside Azerbaijan. Where this document and `shipping-and-delivery.md` or `returns-and-refunds.md` differ, **this document prevails for international orders**. This is rank 3 of the precedence ladder in `returns-and-refunds.md` §8.

1.2 Nothing in `shipping-and-delivery.md` applies to international orders: not the free-shipping threshold, not the domestic zone service levels, not the heavy-item surcharge.

---

## 2. Destinations and zones

| Zone | Destinations | Transit time |
|---|---|---|
| Zone A | Georgia, Türkiye, Kazakhstan, Russia | 5–8 business days |
| Zone B | European Union, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates | 8–14 business days |
| Zone C | all other served destinations | 14–21 business days |

2.1 Transit times are counted in business days from dispatch and exclude time held in customs.

2.2 Aurora Goods does not ship to destinations under Azerbaijani or international trade restriction. The current excluded list is maintained by Logistics and shown at checkout.

---

## 3. Weight, size and value limits

3.1 The maximum shipping weight for a single international parcel is **30.0 kg**. A parcel weighing exactly 30.0 kg is accepted; a parcel above 30.0 kg is not accepted and the order is split or cancelled.

3.2 The maximum declared value for a single international parcel is **5,000.00 AZN**.

3.3 Cash on delivery is not available on any international order; see `payments-and-billing.md` §2.1.

---

## 4. Duties, taxes and shipping charges

4.0 **How order value is measured.** The threshold in §4.2 is measured on **merchandise value**, excluding international shipping charges and any duties.

4.1 International shipping is charged at checkout and is **never free**. There is no international equivalent of the domestic 100.00 AZN free-shipping threshold, and Aurora Plus membership does not provide free international shipping.

4.2 Orders with a merchandise value of **1,000.00 AZN or more** are shipped **DDP**: Aurora Goods prepays destination duties and import taxes, and the customer pays nothing further on delivery.

4.3 Orders below 1,000.00 AZN are shipped **DDU**: the customer is responsible for destination duties, import taxes, and any customs broker fee. These are charged by the destination authority, not by Aurora Goods.

| Merchandise value | Terms | Who pays duties |
|---|---|---|
| 999.99 AZN | DDU | customer |
| 1,000.00 AZN | DDP | Aurora Goods |
| 1,000.01 AZN | DDP | Aurora Goods |

4.4 A parcel refused at customs, or abandoned because the customer declines to pay duties under §4.3, is treated as a refused delivery. Outbound shipping, duties already incurred, and return-to-origin freight are deducted from any refund.

---

## 5. International returns

5.1 The return window for international orders is **21 calendar days** from the delivery date, counted using the same rule as `returns-and-refunds.md` §2.2 (delivery date is day 0).

| Calendar days elapsed since delivery | Within international window? |
|---|---|
| 0 – 20 | Yes |
| 21 | Yes — final day |
| 22 or more | No |

5.2 The 21-day international window replaces the domestic 14-day window, the 30-day Aurora Plus window, and — for international deliveries only — is applied ahead of the 7-day promotional window under rank 3 of the precedence ladder. Clearance and non-returnable categories still take precedence over it.

5.3 Aurora Goods does **not** issue prepaid return labels for international returns. The customer arranges and pays for return carriage, except where the item is defective, damaged in transit, or incorrectly shipped, in which case Aurora Goods reimburses documented return carriage up to **120.00 AZN**.

5.4 The restocking fee in `returns-and-refunds.md` §3.2 applies unchanged.

5.5 Duties and import taxes already paid are not refunded by Aurora Goods. Recovery of duties is a matter between the customer and the destination customs authority.

5.6 The returned parcel must reach the Aurora Goods warehouse within **30 calendar days** of the RMA being issued. Parcels arriving after that are refused.

---

## 6. Transit damage on international orders

6.1 The transit-damage reporting deadline for international orders is **14 calendar days** from delivery, rather than the 7 days in `returns-and-refunds.md` §5.1. The longer period reflects customs handling.

---

## 7. Warranty on international orders

7.1 The warranty periods in `warranty-policy.md` §2 apply worldwide and are not shortened for international customers.

7.2 The customer pays outbound carriage to the Aurora Goods service centre for an international warranty claim. Aurora Goods pays return carriage of the repaired or replaced item.
