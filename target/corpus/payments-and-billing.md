## 1. Accepted payment methods

1.1 Aurora Goods accepts:
- Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards issued in Azerbaijan or abroad;
- Apple Pay and Google Pay;
- bank transfer against a proforma invoice;
- cash on delivery (COD), domestic orders only;
- Aurora Goods gift cards;
- Aurora Goods store credit.

1.2 All prices and all charges are in Azerbaijani manat (AZN). Aurora Goods does not process payments in any other currency. Cards issued abroad are charged in AZN and converted by the issuing bank at its own rate.

---

## 2. Cash on delivery

2.0 **How order value is measured.** Every value threshold in this document is measured on **merchandise value**: the sum of the prices actually paid for the goods, excluding shipping charges, the COD handling fee, and the Aurora Plus membership fee.

2.1 COD is available only for orders delivered inside Azerbaijan.

2.2 COD is available for orders with a total of **500.00 AZN or less**.

| Order total | COD available? |
|---|---|
| 499.99 AZN | Yes |
| 500.00 AZN | Yes |
| 500.01 AZN | No |

2.3 Orders above the COD limit must be paid by card, bank transfer, or wallet before dispatch.

2.4 COD orders carry a handling fee of **3.00 AZN**.

---

## 3. Authorisation and holds

3.1 When an order is placed by card, the issuer places an authorisation hold for the order total. The order is confirmed only once authorisation succeeds; see `shipping-and-delivery.md` §2.4.

3.2 Aurora Goods captures the authorised amount at dispatch, not at order placement.

3.3 If an order is cancelled before dispatch, the authorisation is released. Issuers release holds within **7 calendar days**; Aurora Goods cannot shorten this period.

3.4 An authorisation hold is not a charge and does not appear on a card statement as a completed transaction.

---

## 4. Instalments

4.1 Interest-free instalments are offered on orders of **200.00 AZN or more**, in 3, 6, or 12 monthly instalments.

4.2 Instalment plans are available only on cards issued by partner banks in Azerbaijan, and not with COD.

4.3 If an order paid by instalments is returned, the refund closes the remaining instalment schedule. The customer's bank reverses future instalments within **10 business days**.

---

## 5. Failed and declined payments

5.1 For recurring charges (Aurora Plus renewal), a declined payment is retried up to **3 times**, at **24-hour** intervals.

5.2 After the third failed retry the subscription is suspended. See `account-and-membership.md` §6.

5.3 For one-off orders there is no automatic retry. The order is held as unpaid for **48 hours** and then cancelled, and reserved stock is released.

---

## 6. Refunds

6.1 Refunds are issued to the original payment method. A refund cannot be redirected to a different card or a different person's account.

6.2 Once a refund is approved, Aurora Goods submits it to the payment provider within **1 business day**. The funds then reach the customer according to the method:

| Original payment method | Time to reach customer after submission |
|---|---|
| Card (Visa / Mastercard) | 5–10 business days |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | 5–10 business days |
| Bank transfer | 3–5 business days |
| Cash on delivery | 3–5 business days, to a bank account provided by the customer |
| Store credit | within 24 hours |

6.3 A customer may choose to receive a refund as **store credit** instead of to the original method. Store credit is issued within 24 hours and carries a **5% bonus** on the refunded amount. Store credit does not expire.

6.4 Refunds for returned goods are approved only after warehouse inspection; see `returns-and-refunds.md` §7.2.

6.5 Where a COD order is refunded, the customer must supply an IBAN. Aurora Goods does not refund COD orders in cash.

---

## 7. Invoices and receipts

7.1 An electronic receipt is issued for every order and is available in the customer's order history.

7.2 Receipts are retained and downloadable for **5 years** from the order date; see `privacy-and-data.md` §5.

---

## 8. Chargebacks

8.1 Where a chargeback is opened, the order is frozen and no return, refund, or warranty claim can be processed on it until the chargeback is resolved by the issuer.

8.2 Issuers typically resolve chargebacks within **45 calendar days**. Aurora Goods has no control over this period.
