## 1. Scope

1.1 This document covers domestic delivery only. Deliveries outside Azerbaijan are governed by `international-shipping.md`, which has its own service levels, weight limits, and duty rules.

1.2 All times in this document are Asia/Baku (UTC+04:00). Azerbaijan does not observe daylight saving time, so this offset is constant.

---

## 2. Order cut-off and dispatch

2.1 Orders that are paid and confirmed at or before **14:00** on a business day are dispatched the same business day.

2.2 Orders confirmed after 14:00 are dispatched on the next business day.

| Order confirmation time | Dispatch |
|---|---|
| 13:59 | same business day |
| 14:00 | same business day |
| 14:01 | next business day |

2.3 Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays announced by the Cabinet of Ministers. Orders confirmed on a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday are dispatched on the next business day regardless of the time of confirmation.

2.4 An order is "confirmed" when payment authorisation succeeds, not when the order is submitted. See `payments-and-billing.md` §3 for authorisation timing.

---

## 3. Delivery zones and service levels

3.1 Delivery estimates are counted in **business days from dispatch**, not from order placement.

| Zone | Coverage | Standard delivery | Express delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zone 1 | Baku and Absheron peninsula | 1–2 business days | next business day |
| Zone 2 | Ganja, Sumgait, Mingachevir, Shirvan, Lankaran, Sheki, Nakhchivan city | 2–4 business days | 1–2 business days |
| Zone 3 | all other settlements | 4–7 business days | not available |

3.2 Delivery estimates are estimates, not guarantees. A parcel is treated as **delayed** only once it exceeds the upper bound of its zone estimate by **3 business days**.

3.3 Express delivery is available for orders confirmed before the cut-off in §2.1 and is not available to Zone 3.

---

## 4. Shipping charges

4.0 **How order value is measured.** Every value threshold in this document is measured on **merchandise value**: the sum of the prices actually paid for the goods, excluding shipping charges, express surcharges, heavy-item surcharges, COD handling fees, and the Aurora Plus membership fee.

4.1 Standard shipping is **free** on orders with a paid total of **100.00 AZN or more**.

4.2 Orders with a paid total **below 100.00 AZN** are charged a flat standard shipping fee of **5.90 AZN**.

4.3 Express delivery costs **12.00 AZN** in Zone 1 and **19.00 AZN** in Zone 2, and this charge applies regardless of order value. The free shipping threshold in §4.1 applies only to standard shipping.

4.4 Parcels with a shipping weight **above 30.0 kg** carry a heavy-item surcharge of **25.00 AZN**. Parcels weighing exactly 30.0 kg are not surcharged.

4.5 Aurora Plus members receive free standard shipping with no minimum order value. See `account-and-membership.md` §4.

---

## 5. Signature and high-value orders

5.1 Orders with a paid total **above 750.00 AZN** require an adult signature on delivery and cannot be left with a neighbour or at an unattended address.

5.2 Orders at or below 750.00 AZN may be left at the address without signature if the customer has enabled that option in their account.

---

## 6. Failed delivery attempts

6.1 The carrier makes up to **3 delivery attempts** on consecutive business days.

6.2 After the third failed attempt the parcel is held at the local depot for **5 calendar days**.

6.3 If the parcel is not collected within the hold period, it is returned to the Aurora Goods warehouse. The order is then refunded minus the original outbound shipping charge; the return label fee in `returns-and-refunds.md` §4.2 is not applied.

6.4 A parcel returned to the warehouse under §6.3 cannot be re-sent. The customer must place a new order.

---

## 7. Address changes

7.1 A delivery address can be changed free of charge until the order is dispatched.

7.2 After dispatch, an address change within the same zone costs **7.00 AZN** and is subject to carrier acceptance. Address changes across zones after dispatch are not possible.

---

## 8. Tracking

8.1 A tracking number is issued when the parcel is scanned by the carrier, normally within **4 hours** of dispatch.

8.2 Tracking events are refreshed from the carrier at least every **6 hours**.
