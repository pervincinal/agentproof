## 1. Account creation and access

1.1 An account requires a valid email address and a password of at least **10 characters**.

1.2 After **5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts** the account is locked for **30 minutes**. The counter resets after a successful sign-in or after the lock expires.

1.3 A password reset link is valid for **24 hours** and can be used once.

1.4 Two-factor authentication by SMS or authenticator app is optional and can be enabled at any time.

---

## 2. Aurora Plus membership

2.1 Aurora Plus is an annual paid membership costing **49.00 AZN per year**, charged in advance.

2.2 New members receive a **30-day free trial**. The membership fee is charged on the day after the trial ends unless the trial is cancelled before then.

2.3 One membership covers one account. Membership benefits cannot be shared with other accounts or transferred to another person.

2.4 Membership is "active" for the purpose of any benefit in this document if it was active on the date the relevant order was placed.

---

## 3. Member return window

3.1 Aurora Plus members have an extended return window of **30 calendar days**, counted using the same rule as `returns-and-refunds.md` §2.2 (delivery date is day 0).

| Calendar days elapsed since delivery | Within member window? |
|---|---|
| 0 – 29 | Yes |
| 30 | Yes — final day |
| 31 or more | No |

3.2 The member window applies only where the precedence ladder in `returns-and-refunds.md` §8 reaches rank 5. In particular it does **not** apply to promotional items, to clearance items, to non-returnable categories, or to orders delivered outside Azerbaijan.

3.3 The restocking fee in `returns-and-refunds.md` §3.2 applies to members on the same terms as to non-members. Membership extends the window; it does not waive the fee.

---

## 4. Other membership benefits

4.1 Free standard domestic shipping with **no minimum order value**, replacing the 100.00 AZN threshold in `shipping-and-delivery.md` §4.1. Express delivery charges still apply.

4.2 A **6-month warranty extension** on purchases made while the membership is active; see `warranty-policy.md` §2.3.

4.3 Early access to seasonal campaigns, normally **24 hours** before general release.

4.4 Membership benefits do not include any additional discount on list prices.

---

## 5. Cancelling Aurora Plus

5.1 A membership cancelled within **14 calendar days** of the charge date is refunded in full, provided no member benefit has been used in that period. Placing an order that used free member shipping counts as using a benefit.

5.2 A membership cancelled after the 14-day period is refunded **pro rata for each whole unused month remaining**, calculated from the cancellation date. Partial months are not refunded.

5.3 Benefits end on the cancellation date, not at the end of the paid year. A warranty extension already earned under §4.2 is not withdrawn.

5.4 A membership cancelled and re-taken within **12 months** is not eligible for a further free trial.

---

## 6. Suspension for non-payment

6.1 If the annual renewal charge fails after the retry sequence in `payments-and-billing.md` §5.1, membership is suspended immediately.

6.2 A suspended membership can be reinstated by paying the outstanding fee within **30 calendar days**, and benefits resume without a new trial.

6.3 After 30 days the membership is closed and a new sign-up is required.

---

## 7. Closing an account

7.1 A customer may close their account at any time from account settings.

7.2 Closing an account cancels any active Aurora Plus membership under §5.2 and forfeits unused store credit **only where the customer has confirmed this in writing**. Where no confirmation is given, store credit is retained for the customer for 12 months.

7.3 Closing an account is not the same as erasing personal data. Data erasure is governed by `privacy-and-data.md` §3.
