## 1. Scope and definitions

1.1 This policy governs the return of physical goods purchased from Aurora Goods and delivered to a domestic address. Orders delivered outside Azerbaijan are governed by `international-shipping.md` §5.

1.2 **"Delivery date"** means the date recorded by the carrier as the date the parcel was handed to the customer or to a person at the delivery address. This date is shown in the customer's order history and is the only date used to calculate return windows.

1.3 **"Return window"** means the period during which a customer may *initiate* a return. Initiating a return means submitting a return request through the Aurora Goods website, app, or support channel and receiving a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number.

1.4 **"List price"** means the price at which the item was offered immediately before any discount, promotion, or campaign was applied.

1.5 A return is a request to send goods back for a refund. A warranty claim is a request to repair or replace a defective product after the return window has closed. Warranty claims are governed by `warranty-policy.md`.

---

## 2. Standard return window

2.1 The standard return window is **14 calendar days**.

2.2 **Counting rule.** The delivery date counts as day 0. The window closes at 23:59 (Asia/Baku) on the 14th calendar day after the delivery date. Weekends and public holidays are included in the count.

| Calendar days elapsed since delivery | Within standard window? |
|---|---|
| 0 – 13 | Yes |
| 14 | Yes — final day |
| 15 or more | No |

2.3 Once a return has been initiated, the customer must hand the parcel to the carrier within **5 calendar days** of the RMA being issued. RMAs that are not used within 5 calendar days expire and the return is cancelled.

2.4 The standard return window applies to items that are not promotional, not clearance, not damaged on arrival, and not purchased by an Aurora Plus member. Where more than one rule could apply, use the precedence ladder in §8.

---

## 3. Condition of returned goods and restocking fee

3.1 Returned goods must be complete: the product, all accessories, cables, manuals, and the original manufacturer packaging.

3.2 A **restocking fee of 15%** of the price paid is deducted from the refund when the item is returned opened or unsealed but in resalable condition.

3.3 No restocking fee is charged when:
- the item is returned unopened with the manufacturer seal intact;
- the item is defective, damaged in transit, or not the item that was ordered;
- the return is the result of an Aurora Goods fulfilment error.

3.4 Items returned incomplete, with signs of use beyond reasonable inspection, or without original packaging are refused and returned to the customer at the customer's expense.

---

## 4. Return shipping costs

4.0 **How order value is measured.** The threshold in §4.1 is measured on **merchandise value**: the prices actually paid for the goods, excluding shipping charges and handling fees.

4.1 Orders with a merchandise value of **150.00 AZN or more** receive a free prepaid return label.

4.2 Orders with a merchandise value **below 150.00 AZN** are charged a return label fee of **9.90 AZN**, deducted from the refund.

4.3 Items with a shipping weight **above 20.0 kg** cannot be returned by parcel carrier. Aurora Goods arranges freight collection and deducts a collection fee of **45.00 AZN**. Items weighing exactly 20.0 kg are handled as normal parcels.

4.4 Return shipping is always free, and no restocking fee is charged, when the item is defective, damaged in transit, or incorrectly shipped.

---

## 5. Items damaged, defective, or missing on arrival

5.1 Damage visible on arrival, missing items from a multi-item parcel, and parcels that arrive opened must be reported within **7 calendar days** of the delivery date.

5.2 This 7-day reporting deadline is a **separate obligation** from the return window in §2. It exists because carrier claims must be filed quickly. A customer who reports damage on day 3 and a customer who initiates an ordinary change-of-mind return on day 13 are both acting within their respective deadlines; the two periods do not override one another.

5.3 Damage reported after the 7-day deadline is not refused automatically, but it is handled as a warranty claim under `warranty-policy.md` rather than as a transit damage claim, and photographic evidence is required.

5.4 Reports under this section must include photographs of the item and of the outer packaging.

---

## 6. Items that cannot be returned

6.1 The following are non-returnable in all cases:
- personal care and grooming items once the hygiene seal is broken;
- perishable goods;
- custom-made, personalised, or engraved items;
- digital downloads and software licence keys once the key has been revealed;
- items sold as **Clearance / Final Sale** (see `promotions-and-price-match.md` §4).

6.2 The exclusions in §6.1 take precedence over every other rule in this policy, including the Aurora Plus extension.

---

## 7. Refunds

7.1 Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Refund processing times, store credit options, and failed-refund handling are defined in `payments-and-billing.md` §6.

7.2 Aurora Goods begins refund processing after the returned goods have been received and inspected at the warehouse. Inspection is completed within **3 business days** of receipt.

7.3 Original outbound shipping charges are refunded only when the return is caused by an Aurora Goods error.

---

## 8. Precedence ladder

8.1 When two or more rules could apply to the same item, apply them in this order. The first matching rule decides the return window; lower rules are not applied.

| Rank | Rule | Where defined |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Non-returnable category / Clearance Final Sale | this document §6, `promotions-and-price-match.md` §4 |
| 2 | Damaged, defective, or wrong item on arrival | this document §5 |
| 3 | Delivery address outside Azerbaijan | `international-shipping.md` §5 |
| 4 | Promotional item | `promotions-and-price-match.md` §2 |
| 5 | Aurora Plus member | `account-and-membership.md` §3 |
| 6 | Standard return window (14 days) | this document §2 |

8.2 Two consequences of this ladder are stated explicitly because they are frequently misread:
- An Aurora Plus member who buys a promotional item gets the **promotional** window, not the member window.
- An Aurora Plus member whose parcel is delivered outside Azerbaijan gets the **international** window, not the member window.

**A.1 Return window (superseded 2026-01-01).** Under v3.2 the standard return window was **30 calendar days** from the delivery date.

**A.2 Restocking fee (superseded 2026-01-01).** Under v3.2 the restocking fee for opened but resalable goods was **20%** of the price paid.

**A.3 Transit damage reporting (superseded 2026-01-01).** Under v3.2 transit damage had to be reported within **14 calendar days** of delivery.

**A.4 Return label fee (superseded 2026-01-01).** Under v3.2 return labels were free on all orders regardless of order value.
