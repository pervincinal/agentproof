## 1. Scope and relationship to returns

1.1 A warranty claim is a request to repair or replace a product that has failed in normal use. It is not the same as a return. A customer who simply changed their mind must use `returns-and-refunds.md`; a customer whose product stopped working must use this policy.

1.2 The warranty period runs from the **delivery date**, not from the order date and not from the date the product was first used.

1.3 **Which version applies.** The length of a product's warranty is fixed by the version of this policy that was in force on the **delivery date**, and does not change when this policy is later revised. A product delivered on 2025-06-10 is therefore covered for the period stated in v3.0, not the period stated in v3.1. Service charges that are incurred today — for example the out-of-warranty diagnostic fee in §6.2 — are charged at the current rate. Appendix A exists for this reason and is not decorative.

1.4 A warranty claim can be opened at any point inside the warranty period, including while the return window is still open.

1.5 **Counting rule.** A warranty of N months expires at 23:59 (Asia/Baku) on the day of the month N months after the delivery date that has the same day number as the delivery date. That final day is inside the warranty period. Where the target month has no such day number, the period ends on the last day of that month.

| 2025-08-31 | 12 months | 2026-08-31 |

## 2. Warranty periods

2.1 **Standard warranty: 12 months.** This applies to all third-party branded products sold by Aurora Goods.

2.2 **Aurora-branded products: 24 months.** Products sold under the Aurora, Aurora Home, or Aurora Studio brands carry a 24-month warranty.

2.3 **Aurora Plus extension: +6 months.** An active Aurora Plus membership at the time of purchase adds 6 months to whichever of §2.1 or §2.2 applies. The extension is tied to the purchase, not to the membership: cancelling Aurora Plus later does not remove an extension already earned.

| Product | No membership | Aurora Plus at purchase |
|---|---|---|
| Third-party brand | 12 months | 18 months |
| Aurora brand | 24 months | 30 months |

2.4 **Consumable components: 6 months.** Rechargeable batteries, filters, brush heads, printer cartridges, and lamps are covered for 6 months regardless of the product's headline warranty period and regardless of Aurora Plus membership. The 6-month consumable period is not extended by §2.3.

2.5 Where a product's headline warranty and a consumable component's warranty differ, the component follows §2.4 and the rest of the product follows §2.1 or §2.2.

## 3. What is covered

3.1 Manufacturing defects, component failure in normal domestic use, and firmware faults that prevent the product from performing its advertised function.

3.2 Aurora Goods chooses between repair, replacement with an identical or equivalent product, and refund. The customer cannot require a specific remedy.

---

## 4. What voids or excludes cover

4.1 The warranty does not cover, and the following void cover entirely:
- liquid ingress and water damage, unless the product carries a published IP rating and was used within that rating;
- physical damage from dropping, crushing, or impact;
- repair or disassembly by anyone other than Aurora Goods or an authorised service partner;
- removal, alteration, or obscuring of the serial number;
- use of non-approved power supplies or chargers;
- normal cosmetic wear, scratches, and discolouration;
- commercial or industrial use of a product sold for domestic use.

4.2 Consumable depletion is not a defect. A rechargeable battery that retains at least **80%** of its rated capacity is considered to be performing normally.

---

## 5. Making a claim

5.1 Claims require the order number and a description of the fault. Photographs or a short video are required for physical symptoms.

5.2 After-Sales Service responds to a submitted claim within **3 business days**.

5.3 If the claim is accepted, the customer receives a prepaid shipping label. Assessment at the service centre takes up to **10 business days** from receipt.

5.4 Repaired or replaced products carry the **remainder of the original warranty period, or 3 months, whichever is longer**.

---

## 6. Out-of-warranty service

6.1 Products outside the warranty period may be submitted for paid repair. Aurora Goods provides a quotation before any work begins.

6.2 The diagnostic fee is **25.00 AZN** and is waived if the customer accepts the quotation.
