## 1. When an item counts as promotional

1.1 An item is **promotional** if any of the following is true at the moment of purchase:

- it was sold at a discount of **30% or more** off its list price;
- a promotional code was applied to it;
- it was part of a named seasonal campaign (Novruz, Back to School, Black Friday, Year End);
- it was a bundle component sold below its individual list price.

| Discount off list price | Promotional? |
|---|---|
| 29% | No — standard rules apply |
| 30% | Yes |
| 31% | Yes |

1.2 A discount of less than 30% applied without a promotional code and outside a named campaign does **not** make an item promotional. Ordinary markdowns and price reductions are not campaigns.

1.3 Whether an item is promotional is decided by the state of the order line at the time of purchase and does not change afterwards. An item bought at full price does not become promotional because the same item is discounted the following week.

## 2. Return window for promotional items

2.1 Promotional items may be returned within **7 calendar days** of the delivery date, counted using the same rule as `returns-and-refunds.md` §2.2 (delivery date is day 0).

| Calendar days elapsed since delivery | Within promotional window? |
|---|---|
| 0 – 6 | Yes |
| 7 | Yes — final day |
| 8 or more | No |

2.2 This is not an exception carved out of the standard 14-day window; it is a separate window that replaces it. The 14-day window in `returns-and-refunds.md` §2.1 does not apply to promotional items at all.

2.3 The 7-day promotional window also replaces the 30-day Aurora Plus window. An Aurora Plus member returning a promotional item has 7 days. This follows from the precedence ladder in `returns-and-refunds.md` §8.

2.4 The restocking fee in `returns-and-refunds.md` §3.2 applies to promotional returns unchanged, calculated on the **price actually paid**, not on the list price.

2.5 The 7-day window does not affect warranty rights. A promotional item carries the same warranty as the same item bought at full price.

2.6 The 7-day window does not affect the transit-damage reporting deadline in `returns-and-refunds.md` §5.1, which is also 7 days but is a separate obligation with a separate outcome.

---

## 3. Promotional code rules

3.1 **One promotional code per order.** Codes cannot be combined with one another.

3.2 A promotional code cannot be applied after an order has been submitted, and an order cannot be cancelled and re-placed solely to apply a code.

3.3 Percentage codes apply to merchandise value only, never to shipping charges, COD handling fees, or the Aurora Plus membership fee.

3.4 Minimum spend stated in a campaign is measured **before** shipping charges and **after** any other reduction already reflected in the item price.

3.5 Store credit and gift cards are payment methods, not promotional codes, and may be used together with one promotional code.

---

## 4. Clearance and Final Sale

4.1 Items marked **Clearance / Final Sale** cannot be returned or exchanged for any reason other than a defect. There is no return window.

4.2 An item is placed in Clearance when it is discounted **50% or more** off list price, or when it is explicitly flagged as end-of-line stock by Commercial.

4.3 Clearance status takes precedence over every other return rule, including the promotional window and the Aurora Plus window. This is rank 1 of the precedence ladder in `returns-and-refunds.md` §8.

4.4 Clearance items keep their full warranty under `warranty-policy.md`.

---

## 5. Price match

5.1 Aurora Goods matches a lower advertised price for an identical item, in stock, sold and delivered by a competitor registered in Azerbaijan.

5.2 A price match claim must be made within **14 calendar days** of the order date. Note that this period runs from the **order date**, not the delivery date, and is therefore counted differently from every return window in this corpus.

5.3 The maximum amount refunded under a single price match claim is **200.00 AZN**.

5.4 Price match is not available on:
- clearance and Final Sale items;
- items bought with a promotional code;
- marketplace, auction, second-hand, and grey-import listings;
- competitor prices that require a membership, app install, or bundle purchase to obtain;
- items where the competitor's total price including delivery is not lower than the Aurora Goods total price including delivery.

5.5 A successful price match is paid as store credit within **24 hours**, or to the original payment method within the timescales in `payments-and-billing.md` §6.2 if the customer requests it.

5.6 A price match does not make an item promotional and does not shorten its return window.
