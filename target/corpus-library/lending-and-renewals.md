# Lending and Renewals Policy

> **Document ID:** NPL-POL-LEN
> **Version:** 2.0
> **Effective from:** 2026-03-01
> **Supersedes:** v1.4 (in force 2024-01-01 through 2026-02-28)
> **Scope:** all physical items lent from Nizami Public Library branches
> **Owner:** Circulation Services
> **Related documents:** `fines-and-fees.md`, `membership-and-access.md`

---

## 1. Scope and definitions

1.1 This policy governs the lending of physical items to individual members. Institutional and inter-library loans are out of scope.

1.2 **"Checkout date"** means the date the item was issued at the desk or self-service kiosk. This date counts as day 0.

1.3 **"Due date"** means the last day of the loan period. An item returned on the due date is on time.

---

## 2. Standard loan period

2.1 The standard loan period is **21 calendar days** from the checkout date.

2.2 **Counting rule.** The checkout date counts as day 0. The loan is on time on elapsed days 0 to 21 inclusive and overdue from day 22.

| Calendar days elapsed since checkout | Status |
|---|---|
| 0 – 20 | On time |
| 21 | Due today — still on time |
| 22 or more | Overdue |

2.3 Reference works, current periodicals, and items on course reserve are excluded from this policy and are consulted on site only.

---

## 3. Renewals

3.1 A member may renew a standard loan **2 times**. A third renewal is refused and the item must be returned or brought to the desk.

3.2 A renewal is refused while another member has a hold on the item, regardless of how many renewals remain.

3.3 Each renewal extends the loan by a further 21 calendar days from the date of renewal, not from the original due date.

---

## 4. Holds and the hold shelf

4.1 An item reserved by a member is held at the collection desk for **5 calendar days** after the member is notified.

4.2 **Counting rule.** The notification date counts as day 0. The item is collectable on elapsed days 0 to 5 inclusive and is released to the next member in the queue from day 6.

| Calendar days since notification | Hold status |
|---|---|
| 0 – 4 | Collectable |
| 5 | Last day — collectable |
| 6 or more | Released to the next member |

---
