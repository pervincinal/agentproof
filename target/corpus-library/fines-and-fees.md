# Fines and Fees Policy

> **Document ID:** NPL-POL-FIN
> **Version:** 3.1
> **Effective from:** 2026-05-01
> **Supersedes:** v3.0 (in force 2025-02-01 through 2026-04-30)
> **Scope:** overdue charges, replacement charges, and payment handling
> **Owner:** Circulation Services
> **Related documents:** `lending-and-renewals.md`, `membership-and-access.md`

---

## 1. Overdue charges

1.1 An overdue item accrues a fine of **0.20 AZN** for each calendar day past the due date.

1.2 The total overdue fine for a single item is capped at **10.00 AZN**. Once the accrued amount reaches the cap it stops growing, no matter how long the item stays out.

| Accrued fine on one item | Charged |
|---|---|
| 9.99 AZN | 9.99 AZN — still accruing |
| 10.00 AZN | 10.00 AZN — cap reached |
| above 10.00 AZN | 10.00 AZN — capped |

1.3 Fines are calculated per item, not per loan transaction.

---

## 2. Grace period

2.1 No fine is charged for the first **3 calendar days** after the due date. The grace period runs from day 1 after the due date and includes day 3.

| Calendar days past the due date | Fine |
|---|---|
| 1 – 2 | No fine — within grace |
| 3 | No fine — last grace day |
| 4 or more | Fine applies from day 1 |

2.2 The grace period is applied once per item per loan. It does not reset on renewal.

---

## 3. Replacement charges

3.1 An item declared lost is charged a flat replacement fee of **25.00 AZN** in addition to any accrued overdue fine.

3.2 A replacement fee is refunded in full if the item is returned undamaged within 30 calendar days of the charge.

3.3 Damaged items are assessed by Collections staff; there is no fixed charge.
