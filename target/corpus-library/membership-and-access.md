> **Owner:** Member Services
> **Related documents:** `lending-and-renewals.md`, `fines-and-fees.md`

---

## 1. Eligibility

1.1 A person aged **14 years** or older may hold a membership card in their own name.

| Age at application | Card type |
|---|---|
| 13 or younger | Junior card — guardian signature required |
| 14 | Standard card |
| 15 or older | Standard card |

1.2 Applicants under 14 are issued a junior card, which a guardian must co-sign.

---

## 2. Borrowing allowance

2.1 A member may hold **8 items** on loan at the same time.

| Items already on loan | May borrow another? |
|---|---|
| 7 | Yes |
| 8 | No — allowance reached |
| 9 or more | No |

2.2 The allowance counts items on loan, not items on hold.

---

## 3. Card validity

3.1 A membership card is valid for **12 months** from the date of issue and is renewed free of charge at any branch.

| Months since issue | Card status |
|---|---|
| 11 | Valid |
| 12 | Valid — final month |
| 13 or more | Expired — renewal required |

3.2 An expired card can be renewed at any time; borrowing history is retained.
